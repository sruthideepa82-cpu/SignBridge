# Consent form template: ISL landmark recording for SignBridge

**Draft for ethics review. Do not use with participants until your institution's ethics committee or supervisor has approved the final wording.**

## What this study is

SignBridge is a student capstone project. It is building a tool that recognizes a small set of Indian Sign Language (ISL) hand signs from a webcam. To train and test that tool, we record the positions of your hand joints (21 points per hand), not your face, body, or voice.

## What we will record

- Hand joint positions as numbers, for example the position of the wrist and fingertips in each frame.
- The label of the sign you are making, the date, and the camera settings.
- A pseudonym (for example P001) in place of your name.

We will **not** record or store video, photos, audio, or your name in the dataset.

## What you will do

- Make the signs listed in the session plan, one at a time, in front of a webcam.
- Each session should take about [X] minutes. You may take breaks at any time.
- You may be asked to sign again in a later session on a different day.

## Risks and limits

- Using the camera for a while can be tiring. You can stop at any time.
- Joint positions could in principle identify a person, so we store them separately from your name and keep them only for this project.
- This tool is not a sign-language interpreter and is not used to make decisions about anyone. It is a student prototype.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can decline or stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask us to delete all your recordings. We will do so using your pseudonym and our consent reference.
- You can ask to see what we have stored about you.
